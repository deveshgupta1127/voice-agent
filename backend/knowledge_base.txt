Horizon Bank offers card blocking services. If your debit card or credit card is lost, stolen, or you notice suspicious activity on your card, we can block it immediately. Once a card is blocked, it cannot be unblocked. A replacement card will be issued and delivered to your registered address within 5 to 7 working days. You will receive a blocking reference number for your records.

Horizon Bank can help you check your account balance. You can ask for the balance of your savings account or current account. If you have multiple accounts, we can check the balance for any of them.

Horizon Bank provides transaction history. You can view your recent transactions including credits, debits, ATM withdrawals, UPI payments, NEFT transfers, salary credits, and online shopping. We can show you the last 5, 10, or more transactions.

If you have a failed transaction or an unexpected charge on your account, Horizon Bank can help. We can check the status of any transaction to see if it was completed, failed, or reversed. If a transaction failed, the amount should not have been deducted from your account.

Horizon Bank allows you to raise a dispute for problematic transactions. You can dispute a transaction if it was a failed transaction where money was deducted, an unauthorized charge you did not make, a duplicate charge where you were charged twice, or an incorrect amount that does not match what you expected. You will receive a dispute reference number and the bank will investigate within 7 to 10 working days.

Horizon Bank can help with bill payments. You can pay your electricity bill, water bill, gas bill, insurance premium, or any other pending bill directly from your bank account. We will show you all your pending bills and you can choose which one to pay.

Horizon Bank supports EMI payments. If you have a home loan, personal loan, car loan, or education loan, you can pay your monthly EMI through us. We can also show you your loan details including outstanding balance, EMI amount, interest rate, and next due date.

Horizon Bank can help with account access issues. If your netbanking is locked, your debit card PIN is blocked, your account is frozen, or your KYC has expired or is pending, we can check the status and guide you on how to resolve it.

Horizon Bank offers cheque stopping service. If you have issued a cheque and want to prevent it from being encashed, we can stop it for you. You will need to provide the cheque number and amount for verification. A stop cheque reference number will be provided. Note that cheques that are already cleared or already stopped cannot be stopped again.

Horizon Bank does not offer fund transfers to other people through this helpline. For fund transfers, please use netbanking, the mobile app, or visit your nearest branch.

Horizon Bank does not process new account opening through this helpline. To open a new savings account, current account, or any other account, please visit your nearest Horizon Bank branch with your KYC documents.

Horizon Bank does not handle credit card applications through this helpline. To apply for a new credit card, please visit the Horizon Bank website or your nearest branch.

Horizon Bank does not provide investment services through this helpline. For mutual funds, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, or other investment products, please visit your nearest branch or use the mobile app.

Horizon Bank does not sell insurance products through this helpline. For new insurance policies, please contact your nearest branch or visit the Horizon Bank website.

If you are facing an issue that our automated system cannot handle, we can connect you to a human agent who will assist you further. The human agent can help with complex issues, special requests, or any situation where you need personalized attention.
